# The bot knows only a curated public Profile, not the full Career record

The bot answers from a Profile checked into this (public) repo, derived from Nathan's private Career record and reviewed by him, rather than from the Career record itself. The Career record holds salary answers, coaching notes, and private reasoning that a Visitor could extract through the chat, and a leak can't be taken back. Serving the public resume alone was rejected as too thin to beat reading the PDF.

## Consequences

- Two files to keep in sync; a Profile regeneration step (for Nathan's review) is a natural later addition.
- Because the Profile is public, anyone can check the bot's answers against their source.
